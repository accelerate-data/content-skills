# Issue guide

Structure verified against GTM-114 and GTM-117 on 2026-09-09. Read the live examples
on each run for relevant changes; the current opportunity's official sources win
over old program details. Use **VibeData** for the product and **Accelerate Data**
for the company. Do not copy old amounts, eligibility claims, deadlines, or fit.

## Program, branding, and partner route — GTM-114 shape

Title: `Apply: <program and cycle>` or an accurate action such as
`Engage: <partner and route>`.

Use these sections in order:

1. **Submit VibeData application** — or the matching engagement objective.
   Official route URL, current cycle/status, exact deadline/time zone, short
   program summary, and research date. Flag rolling, closed, or unknown status.
2. **Eligibility / fit** — source-backed criteria, matched company facts, and
   unresolved conditions. Include stage, funding history, geography, and required
   commitments when relevant.
3. **Economics** — funding, fees, equity/instrument, benefits, and time costs.
   State unpublished terms as unknown.
4. **Application process** — verified steps, interviews, decision process, form
   inventory below, and access gaps. For a route without a form, describe the
   actual contact/submission mechanism and mark the form as not applicable.
5. **Prepare before submitting** — a short checklist. Link the consolidated
   prerequisite when one exists; keep its detailed preparation checklist there.
6. **Why this fits VibeData** — specific value, positioning, supporting company
   sources, and counterarguments. Distinguish current capabilities from plans.
7. **Decision point** — pursue/conditional/later/reject, eligibility gates,
   prerequisites or future trigger, apply-or-skip condition, and next action.
   Define completion as a recorded outcome or explicit decision, not preparation
   alone. The skill creates the work; a separate task executes submissions.

Cite the official sources beside the claims, with a compact source list if useful.

## Funding route — GTM-117 shape

Title: `Submit: <funding route> — <VibeData purpose>` or
`Engage: <investor> — <stage or trigger>`.

1. **Objective** — assess and pursue the specific funding mechanism through its
   current route; state beneficiary, cycle, status, deadline/time zone, and research
   date. Make the desired outcome explicit.
2. **Why VibeData may fit** — criteria versus verified company facts; technical
   novelty, maturity, legal/ownership conditions, and counterarguments as relevant.
   Include recommendation, unknowns, and a go/no-go gate or future trigger.
3. **Positioning** — the sourced VibeData value and defensibility case appropriate
   to this funder. Do not claim integration of third-party models, prompts, skills,
   dbt, or dlt is proprietary IP. Do not describe already completed work as future
   grant-funded work. Distinguish demonstrated capability from intended research.
4. **Actions** — qualification, preparation, submission mechanism, and outcome
   tracking. Include the form inventory, funding economics and obligations, linked
   prerequisites, next action, and completion condition. Use short subheadings to
   keep this readable. Never imply the skill will submit or contact a funder.
5. **Source** — official opportunity and application URLs, plus company/product
   evidence used for claims. Do not assert vendor approval from customer-project
   funding eligibility.

## Form inventory

Put this inside the application process/actions section. Only include observed
fields. Repeat exact question wording, options, units, and limit values when
visible; use `unknown` for details the page does not disclose.

| Step / exact question or upload | Required? | Options / limit / format | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

Below the table state the inspected steps, inaccessible steps and access obstacle,
and the resulting coverage limit. List suggested preparation separately. Distinguish
an inaccessible questionnaire from a route that has no questionnaire. Do not
present a generic startup application checklist as discovered form questions.

## Consolidated prerequisite

Title: `Prepare / qualify: <opportunity and cycle> — VibeData`.

- **Objective:** the readiness outcome that enables the linked submission.
- **Current blockers to resolve:** facts, access gaps, decisions, and missing
  deliverables supported by research. Distinguish unknown eligibility from proven
  ineligibility.
- **Actions:** one checklist covering the necessary preparation; source and reuse
  existing material. Each item has an observable completion condition. Multiple
  documents or questions stay in this checklist.
- **Ready when:** mandatory criteria are verified, required assets meet known
  limits, and material decisions are recorded. If eligibility fails, record the
  no-go recommendation; do not mark the submission ready automatically.
- **Blocks:** link the submission and set the actual Linear relation after approval.
- **Sources:** supporting opportunity and company evidence.

For a genuinely separate blocker, use the same short structure and explain why it
needs independent tracking. Existing GTM-119 blocking GTM-126 is the sibling-issue
precedent. A future revenue threshold alone is a trigger, not a preparation issue.
